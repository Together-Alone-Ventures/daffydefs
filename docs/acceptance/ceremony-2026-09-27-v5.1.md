- `record_id` is 32 bytes: `75eb570f…5287`. A raw Internet Identity principal is
  29 bytes, so the II principal is not the public `record_id`.
- `record_id` is not derived from any user identifier. The factory drew it from
  `raw_rand` at profile creation (DD2 §3).
- The owner principal behind this profile is deliberately not recorded here.
  DD2 §7 forbids a public principal ↔ profile-canister mapping.

  pending copy of `26c4721c` was downloaded. The pending-absence rule was checked
